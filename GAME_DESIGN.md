# ABYSSAL WAKE Game Design

## Product identity

**ABYSSAL WAKE / 深渊苏醒** is a top-down pixel-art co-op survival sandbox RPG set in a modern post-apocalyptic world touched by cosmic horror. It is about preparing a home, risking expeditions into changed places, surviving what lives there, bringing value back, and gradually making a small human foothold feel real.

The reference set is experiential, not material to copy:

- **Crashlands**: immediate, readable combat feedback and strange creature energy.
- **Terraria**: exploration, equipment-driven access, vertical discovery and boss rhythm.
- **Minecraft**: player expression through building and a base that matters.
- **Don't Starve**: legible survival pressure and a hostile world beyond safety.
- **Stardew Valley**: warmth, routine, growth, farming/life value and community.

## Four pillars

| Pillar | Player promise | Design test |
|---|---|---|
| Adventure | Leave safety to discover places, threats and useful salvage. | Does the trip create a meaningful choice or discovery? |
| Home & Life | Return to a place that gets safer, warmer and more personal. | Does this make the next expedition better or the world feel lived in? |
| Survival | Read and answer pressure from health, hunger, sanity, time and danger. | Is the threat telegraphed and counterplayable rather than random punishment? |
| Progression | Earn new capability through equipment, knowledge, crafting and world stages. | Does progression open a new decision, place or combat possibility? |

## The emotional map: HOME and OUTSIDE

**HOME** begins as SafeCamp and grows toward a player base and settlement. It is the place to recover, organize, craft, meet survivors and see tangible results of previous risk. Its light, music, color and interactions should feel humane.

**OUTSIDE** is a modern world with recognizable remnants—apartments, clinics, transit, industrial sites, convenience stores, roads and utility infrastructure—made unsettling through targeted abyssal transformation. Corruption should be anchored to places and events: impossible growth through concrete, altered signage, abnormal weather, disturbed soundscape and creatures with readable behaviors. It must not be a blanket “dark/purple” filter.

## Primary loop

```text
Prepare at HOME
  → choose an OUTSIDE destination / POI / underground route
  → explore, fight, survive and salvage
  → return with resources, clues and loot
  → craft, build, recover and improve the home
  → reach a harder region, world stage or boss gate
  → repeat with new choices
```

Co-op should amplify roles and stories in this loop—scouting, fighting, carrying, building, reviving and sharing discovery—without making a solo player’s state invalid.

## Design rules for major systems

### Combat and creatures

Combat is the primary moment-to-moment anchor. Attacks need a clear wind-up, hit confirmation, readable danger, knockback/impact language and a tactical reason to move. Monster patterns should be understandable before they are complex. Equipment, pets, base preparation and survival supplies should strengthen combat choices, not replace them.

### World, POIs and underground discovery

The first useful world scale is a handful of memorable, authored POIs around a home area, not an enormous empty map. Surface exploration uses roads, landmarks, hazards and salvage. Underground spaces use top-down rooms, mine/utility routes, resource pockets and escalating risk. Each POI should answer: why go there, what danger changes the route, what comes home, and what it unlocks.

### Home, building and settlement

Building starts with practical, small-scale decisions: safe rest, storage, work surfaces, processing and simple defense. The goal is “a base that changes preparation,” not a broad construction catalog. Later settlement systems introduce survivors, service roles, shared projects, trade and defense only after the player has a meaningful home loop.

### Survival and life

HP, hunger and sanity form the current seed. Future pressure—weather, calendar, temperature, raids, food preservation—must add preparation choices and readable warnings. Farming, fishing, cooking and animals belong to the HOME economy: they should reduce specific expedition pressures or create ingredients for meaningful choices, not become disconnected minigames.

### Progression and world awakening

Progression has three layers: survivor capability (skills/knowledge), equipment and tools, and world condition. World stages represent the abyss becoming more awake: changed POIs, hazards, enemy behavior and boss access. The first boss is a progression test and a memorable world event, not just a larger health bar.

### Narrative, social and mystery

Story is environmental and interactive: broken systems, survivor memories, altered landmarks and short discoveries. Avoid a long forced plot. NPCs, quests, relationships, economy, friends/presence/invites/chat and community are later systems that should create practical co-op and home-life choices.

## Current V21 slice versus target

V21 currently proves only a small foundation: SafeCamp, local player state, basic survival values, base combat/mobs, a Camp-adjacent terrain slice, mobile input, local saves and Relay multiplayer plumbing. It does **not** yet prove the complete HOME/OUTSIDE loop, persistent private worlds, building, farming, pets, bosses, full map exploration, accounts or settlement life. The roadmap is deliberately staged to close that gap without stacking unfinished systems.
