## Findings

The material is sparse and flat beside the user's densely shaded concept. Do not use this atlas as the final replacement.

- **Camp props: REVISE.** The warm wood/teal cloth palette is coherent, but several props rely on flat planes and simple highlights. Their visual richness is below the reference scene; runtime scale and integration remain unverified.
- **Seven MIRE MART blocker images:** separate preview-only batch; they do not replace the SafeCamp lawn, player, or camp props.

## Recommended next step

Make one coherent SafeCamp screen-slice art draft: terrain, one nearby prop, and the player at their native asset contracts. Review the three together at native size and nearest-neighbor scale before expanding the atlas. The current drafts remain intact for A/B comparison; no deletions are recommended.
