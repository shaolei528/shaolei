# Draft content candidate — MIRE MART signal

**Status:** DRAFT — awaiting user choice

**Module lead:** World & Terrain

**Coordinates with:** Progression & Narrative, Combat & Creatures, Rendering & Art, Audio & Feel

**Runtime status:** Not implemented; this is a design proposal only.

## Player-facing idea

On the first short trip from HOME toward MIRE MART, a damaged emergency sign/light produces a brief, irregular signal. The player can notice it from the road, choose whether to investigate, and find a small human trace inside the store. The scene starts as a believable infrastructure failure; one restrained detail suggests that the signal is responding to the player rather than simply malfunctioning.

## Intended experience

- Make the first POI feel recognizable and worth approaching.
- Let the player choose between a quicker safe route and a short optional detour.
- Give the return trip a small, understandable reward such as a practical supply and a clue for a later visit.
- Keep the first anomaly subtle. No full-scene purple treatment, forced combat, or lore dump.

## Visual and sound brief

Use a readable modern pixel-art storefront, a damaged sign, a few salvageable shelves and one restrained light/signal cue. Keep the environment legible in daylight; reserve colder color and stronger sound distortion for the brief anomaly. Any generated image is concept/reference only until separately approved and passed through the art pipeline.

## Questions for the user

1. Keep, edit or discard this candidate?
2. Should the first signal feel mostly human (a trapped survivor), mostly environmental (a failing electrical loop), or uncertain?

## Design risks and gates

- Keep the detour optional and short so it does not block the first expedition loop.
- Do not imply a working radio, quest, persistent POI state, NPC or new runtime semantic exists today.
- Before implementation, check the current MIRE MART scene contract and production-art readiness. Do not bind unvalidated preview art as production content.
